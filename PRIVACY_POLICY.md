# BrandSight Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: January 1, 2024**  
**Last Updated: January 1, 2024**

## 1. Introduction

BrandSight ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Shopify application BrandSight (the "Service").

This policy applies to all merchants and their customers whose data is processed through our Service. By installing and using BrandSight, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Collected from Merchants

When you install BrandSight, we collect:

**Account Information:**
- Shopify store domain and ID
- Store owner name and email address
- Store configuration settings
- Billing information (processed securely through Shopify)

**Store Data:**
- Product information (names, SKUs, vendors, prices, inventory)
- Order data (order details, customer information, payment amounts)
- Customer data (names, email addresses, order history)
- Analytics data (traffic sources, conversion metrics)
- Vendor/brand information

### 2.2 Information Collected from Store Customers

We process the following customer information on behalf of merchants:
- Name and contact information
- Order history and transaction details
- Product interactions and preferences
- Geographic location (country, region)

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

**Technical Data:**
- IP addresses
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Operating system
- Access times and dates
- API usage metrics

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on the following legal grounds:

- **Contractual Necessity**: To provide the Service you've requested
- **Legitimate Interests**: To improve our Service and prevent fraud
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **Consent**: Where explicitly provided by users

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

### 4.1 Provide and Maintain the Service
- Generate analytics and reports on vendor performance
- Calculate metrics (AOV, conversion rates, revenue)
- Synchronize data between Shopify and BrandSight
- Process and display historical data

### 4.2 Improve and Optimize
- Enhance Service features and functionality
- Develop new analytics capabilities
- Optimize performance and user experience
- Conduct research and analysis

### 4.3 Communicate with You
- Send Service-related notifications
- Respond to support requests
- Provide updates about new features
- Send billing and account information

### 4.4 Ensure Security and Compliance
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Monitor for security threats
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 We DO NOT:
- Sell personal data to third parties
- Share data with competitors
- Use customer data for marketing without consent
- Transfer data outside our Service except as described below

### 5.2 We MAY share data with:

**Service Providers:**
- Cloud hosting providers (for data storage)
- Analytics services (aggregated data only)
- Customer support tools
- Payment processors (through Shopify)

**Legal Requirements:**
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect rights, property, or safety
- To prevent fraud or security issues
- With your explicit consent

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1 Storage Location
- Primary data storage: United States
- Backup storage: Multiple geographic regions for redundancy
- All data transfers use encrypted connections

### 6.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures
- PCI DSS compliance for payment data

### 6.3 Data Retention
- Active account data: Retained while account is active
- Inactive accounts: Data deleted after 90 days of inactivity
- Backup data: Retained for 30 days after deletion
- Legal holds: Data retained as required by law

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

### 7.1 You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Restriction**: Limit processing of your data
- **Objection**: Object to certain types of processing
- **Withdraw Consent**: Where processing is based on consent

### 7.2 How to Exercise Your Rights
Contact us at privacy@brandsight.app with:
- Your store domain
- Specific right you wish to exercise
- Verification information

We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

## 8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

**Note**: We do not sell personal information.
